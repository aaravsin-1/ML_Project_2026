DatasetQuestionAnswer
SyntheticDoes the pipeline work and avoid leakage?Yes. LightGBM finds the planted signal (0.584); leakage checks pass.
FI-2010Does our DeepLOB match the paper?Within 0.7–2.3 points. Simple models win at 10–20 events, DeepLOB at 50–100.
Coinbase BTCPredictable? Profitable?Slightly predictable (0.467 vs 0.333 random). Not profitable: about 0.5 bp edge vs 8 bp fees.
BTC + ETHDoes another coin help?A little: +0.016 macro-F1 at 10 s, on all 4 segments.
LOBSTERDoes tick size matter?Yes. Large-tick INTC and MSFT are predictable at 1–5 s; small-tick AMZN and GOOG mostly are not.
Optiver RVPDo winning Kaggle features leak the future?Yes. Only about 59% of their gain survives with past-only data.
Optiver CloseCan ML predict the closing auction?About 2.4% better than predicting zero; retraining helps a little.
