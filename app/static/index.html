Why Average Accuracy Looks Low (~46%): Selective Conviction Curve

Most seconds are balanced noise; filtering by Hybrid Ensemble max probability unlocks 74–89% precision

1. Why Wrong on Average? (±α Noise + Closing Stall)

Over 62%–88% of errors occur right next to the ±α dead-zone boundary (|r₂₀ₛ|/α ≈ 1.2× vs 2.7× when correct). Adding volatility also fixed a hidden late-day freeze bug (~15:15–15:30 IST zero-volatility queues), lifting accuracy from 53.4% → 84.8% on RELIANCE and 46.4% → 73.5% on IDEA.

2. Why TLOB Wins vs Why LightGBM Wins

TLOB wins (17–19% solo) during calmer, lower-volatility buildup where baseline LightGBM only saw 5 s of lags vs TLOB's 100 s attention window. LightGBM wins (19–25% solo) when L1 order flow flips suddenly (32.3% imbalance reversals vs TLOB inertia) or during price rallies.

3. How Multi-Scale v2 + SSF + Hybrid Solved Both

Giving LightGBM 13 causal 10s–60s flow/volatility/clock features + 8 Single-Stock Futures (SSF) features + 10s/20s horizon averaging pushes RELIANCE to 0.516 F1 / 0.708 AUC with TLOB and wins on 25/25 NSE stocks.

Two-Branch ML Architecture

Aligned apples-to-apples evaluation

Branch A feeds 25–27 strictly causal engineered features to CPU models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM + 10.5 µs C++). Branch B feeds raw 100 × 20/40 windows into GPU neural nets (LSTM, CNN+Inception DeepLOB5, TLOB Transformer). Both predict identical held-out timestamps.

Why Block PCA on Order Flow?

Targeted factor extraction without mixing units

Instead of squashing unrelated features together, we fit mean-centred Block PCA strictly on Training multi-level OFI (extracting PC1–PC3). On 10-level Bitcoin it compresses levels 6–10; on 5-level NSE it adds rotated combinations (80.5% variance on RELIANCE) so axis-aligned trees make oblique-like splits.

Covariate Shift Resilience

NIFTY Segment 3 (+10.7% price rally)

When prices rallied from ₹22,540 (Train) to ₹24,949 (Test), static scaling pushed raw inputs out of distribution, dropping LSTM (0.184) and DeepLOB5 (0.197) to coin-flip AUC (0.50). TLOB (+ BiN, 0.439) and ratio-based LightGBM (0.432) survived with zero drop.
